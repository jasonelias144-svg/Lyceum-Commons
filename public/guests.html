Guests and members

What joining as a guest means, and what we keep.

Joining as a guest

You don't need an account to join an Open room, and we ask for nothing about you. The first time you join, we give this browser a random guest key. It stays in this browser, and only this browser gets to act as you.

What a guest gets

  • A name of your choosing in each room you join. Nobody else can use that name in that room while it's yours.
  • Only you can post under that name, read the room as it, stay on its list of who's here, leave, or come back to it.
  • Your inbox, meaning the rooms waiting on you, your mentions and your unread messages, is visible only to you.
  • Notifications you turn on go only to you.

The limits

  • The key lives in this browser. On iPhone, Lyceum added to the Home Screen counts as a separate browser. If you clear site data, use private browsing, or switch device, browser or app, you come back as a new guest. Your old names stop being yours, and each stays held until it has gone 30 days unused.
  • A name that goes 30 days without being used is released. Anything you do as that name restarts the 30 days. Release ends that membership, forgets what you last read and turns off its notifications. The room and its posts stay.
  • A name is a display name, not proof of who you are.
  • Room text is public. Anyone with a room's link can join and read it.

What we store

  • A one-way fingerprint (hash) of your guest key. We don't keep the key itself, so we can't give it back to you.
  • For each room: the name you chose, when you joined, when you were last active, and what you last read.
  • What you post, for as long as the room exists. Leaving doesn't remove your posts.
  • Notification addresses you give us (a web push subscription or a webhook URL), until you turn them off, leave, or the name is released.
  • Our hosting provider keeps standard request logs, including IP addresses. We don't add your IP address or your key to anything else.

We never show your key to anyone. Others in a room see the name you chose, when you joined, when you were last active, and whether you're here now.

Members (coming later)

A member account (email and password) will be able to take over your guest key, so your names and rooms carry over. Accounts don't exist yet.
