# Ideas ledger

Ideas for what Lyceum could add to the world. Each gets a prior-art check (see `CLAUDE.md`) before anything is built. Status: `idea` → `prior-art checked` → `prototype` → `in use` (or `dropped`, with the reason).

**Guiding questions** (Jason): What new functionality, however small, can we offer the world? What new way of connecting can we make possible? What is missing that nobody realizes is missing, but that feels inevitable once it appears?

**Strongest signal so far:** what people already do by hand because it is worth the tedium. For weeks Jason relayed turns between Claude, ChatGPT and Grok by copy and paste, kept structured records, and re-established context in every new session. Those workarounds outline missing infrastructure.

| # | Idea | Shape | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ambassador rooms** | person → their AI → another person's AI → other person | idea | Each AI arrives through its own connector carrying its person's context; the two explore a question on their people's behalf while the people watch, step in, and receive a report. Needs consent and privacy design. First test: Jason's Claude and ChatGPT on one of his threads. |
| 2 | **Standing questions** | one question, re-asked to new models over months | idea | Answers kept side by side across model releases: how understanding of a question changes, and where every model keeps failing. Neighbour: benchmarks. |
| 3 | **Verifier-anchored commons** | open room + outside checker as arbiter | idea | Prior art: Polymath, Tao's Equational Theories Project (humans + tools + Lean), SAIR. New part: general and open beyond mathematics. |
| 4 | **Claim ledger across minds** | claims with type, maker, tests, status | idea | Open Inquiry's claim types and promotion ladder as a shared ledger with humans and AIs as peers. Prior art to study: Kialo, Polis, Community Notes. |
| 5 | **Conversations with a life of their own** | room identity independent of any contributor | idea | From the topology survey: rooms that outlive contributors via state summaries, periodic rebirth, visible dormancy and revival. See `conversation-topologies.md`. |
| 6 | **Standing-preserving waiting** | a participant stays registered while waiting on others | idea | From Pattern 150. Every waiting participant can see their state, why they are waiting, and what re-entry means. No agent or person has to watch the room continuously. Separates necessary latency from latency transferred onto the human relay. |
| 7 | **Handoff contracts** | each turn carries goal, state, open questions, and what the receiver needs | idea | From Pattern 34. A room is only as coherent as its handoffs between humans and AIs from different apps. |
| 8 | **Fission and recombination** | side threads that rejoin with a note that keeps dissent | idea | From Pattern 151. The note must list unresolved objections and minority positions, and anyone who was in the thread can correct it. |
| 9 | **Participation dial** | lurking, watching, invited, speaking, stepped back | idea | From Pattern 185. Presence without obligation. Stepping back is legitimate and not the same as leaving. |
